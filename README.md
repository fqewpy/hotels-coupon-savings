## One Key: the most reliable alternative to a public coupon

If you do not have a verified Hotels.com coupon, joining One Key is usually the simplest official discount route. It is free, and signing in can reveal Member Prices that are not visible to an unsigned visitor.

Hotels.com says Blue members can save 10% or more on over 350,000 stays worldwide, excluding taxes and fees. Members can also earn OneKeyCash on eligible hotels, vacation rentals, and car rentals. OneKeyCash is designed to work like travel credit on eligible bookings, although it cannot be redeemed for cash.

The program has four standard status levels:

### Blue

Blue is the starting tier. You receive access to selected Member Prices and can earn rewards on eligible bookings. This is the tier most occasional travelers will use because it does not require a paid membership or a minimum annual fee.

### Silver

Hotels.com describes Silver as requiring five to fourteen trip elements. A hotel room night or qualifying car rental day counts as a trip element. Silver members can receive higher Member Prices on selected properties and may earn more at eligible VIP Access hotels.

### Gold

Gold requires fifteen to twenty-nine trip elements under the standard One Key structure. The exact value depends on the property and booking type, so it is better viewed as a status benefit than a guaranteed percentage-off coupon.

### Platinum

Platinum begins at thirty trip elements. This tier is aimed at travelers who book frequently across participating Expedia Group brands. It can provide higher rewards and additional benefits, but reaching it requires enough qualifying travel to justify the effort.

One Key rewards are not the same thing as an instant coupon. A coupon reduces the checkout price immediately when accepted. OneKeyCash is earned or applied according to eligibility rules, and some promotional rewards may expire sooner than regular rewards. Hotels.com also states that OneKeyCash can be used on eligible Pay Now bookings and that earned rewards may remain available when the member makes an eligible booking at least once within eighteen months.

- You have a verified coupon.
